Application policy

Privacy policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Personal Drive Backup is a personal file backup utility maintained by Dacheng Shu (ShuDacheng). This policy explains how its rclone and restic workflow accesses and handles Google Drive data.

1. Data accessed

With the account owner’s authorization, the utility accesses Google Drive files, folders and metadata needed for configured operations. This can include file contents, names, folder identifiers, sizes, timestamps and checksums. File contents can include documents, project files, task data and backup archives selected by the owner.

The Google Drive permission requested by rclone can permit reading and managing files throughout the authorized Drive account. Configured commands and paths determine the files actually processed. OAuth access and refresh tokens are used to authenticate those operations.

2. How data is used

Data is used to upload and maintain backups, inspect available versions, verify transfers, download files and restore saved data. Credentials are used to obtain authorized access to Google Drive. Operational logs may contain file paths, sizes, timestamps and error information used to maintain or troubleshoot the workflow.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Access to Google data is limited to operating and maintaining the backup, transfer and restore features described on this website.

3. Storage and protection

The utility runs in a computer or cloud environment managed by the owner. Local source files, configuration, caches and logs reside in that environment; transferred data and backups reside in the owner’s chosen Google Drive storage.

OAuth credentials are stored in private configuration and should be protected with restricted access. Restic encrypts the contents of its backup repository. Direct rclone file transfers are not automatically encrypted by restic and depend on storage protections and any encryption separately configured by the owner.

Folders shared through Google Drive are accessible according to their Drive sharing permissions. The owner is responsible for choosing appropriate permissions and protecting encryption passwords and recovery material.

4. Transfers and third parties

The backup workflow transfers data between the owner-managed environment and Google Drive. Google, the chosen cloud environment provider and any owner-configured infrastructure process data as needed to provide their respective services. Access is governed by their policies and the owner’s configuration.

The public website and its source are hosted by GitHub Pages. Backup contents and OAuth credentials are not published as website content. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These static pages add no analytics scripts, advertising or cookies.

5. Retention and deletion

Files and backup snapshots remain in local storage or Google Drive until the owner removes them using the relevant storage or backup tools. Retention depends on the owner’s backup policy and the services’ retention and trash behavior. Local caches, logs and credential files remain until removed by the owner.

Deleting a backup snapshot may require the backup tool’s normal cleanup process before unreferenced repository data is removed. Revoking authorization does not automatically delete existing files, backups or local copies.

6. Revoking Google access

The owner can revoke this utility’s access from Google Account third-party connections. The owner can also stop the utility, remove its local credentials and separately delete stored files or backups. After access is revoked, a new Google authorization is required for operations that need that access.

7. Google API Limited Use

Personal Drive Backup’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Contact and changes

For questions about data handling or deletion, contact Dacheng Shu through the project’s GitHub issue tracker or the support email displayed on the Google authorization screen. Use public issues only for non-sensitive questions; do not post tokens, passwords or private files.

Updates to this policy will be published at this address with a revised effective date. Material changes in the use of Google data will be disclosed before that changed use, with renewed consent where required.
