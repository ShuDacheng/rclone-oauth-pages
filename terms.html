Effective date: 24 October 2026

These terms describe the personal backup utility maintained by Dacheng Shu (ShuDacheng), using rclone and restic with Google Drive.

1. Intended use

Personal Drive Backup is an owner-operated utility for transferring, backing up and restoring files that the owner is authorized to access. It is provided for personal use. Use it in accordance with applicable laws, Google’s service terms and the terms of the software and infrastructure involved.

2. Authorization and responsibility

Google Drive access requires explicit authorization from the account owner. The owner chooses the storage locations and commands, manages sharing permissions and remains responsible for the content being processed.

Keep OAuth credentials, encryption passwords and recovery information secure. Review backup and transfer commands before running them, and verify that required data can be restored. Losing an encryption password may prevent recovery of encrypted backups.

3. Availability and backups

The utility is provided on an “as is” and “as available” basis, to the extent permitted by applicable law. Availability, transfer speed and successful recovery depend on configuration, network connectivity, service quotas, storage availability and the integrity of the relevant files and credentials.

Keep appropriate independent copies of important data. A completed upload does not replace a restore test. These terms do not exclude rights or protections that cannot lawfully be excluded.

4. Third-party services and software

Google Drive and GitHub operate under their own terms and privacy policies. The open-source rclone and restic projects retain their own licenses and maintainers. This independently maintained personal utility does not imply endorsement by those projects or by Google.

5. Privacy and stopping use

Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy. The owner may stop using the utility and revoke its access at any time through Google Account settings. Stored backups, local copies and credentials must be managed separately; revocation does not delete them automatically.

6. Contact and updates

Contact the maintainer through the project’s GitHub issue tracker or the support email shown on the Google authorization screen. Changes to these terms will be posted here with an updated effective date.
